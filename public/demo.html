How MedCard Works
1️⃣ Connect & Authorize: Securely connect your healthcare providers (hospitals, pharmacies, labs, payers) via FHIR APIs federally mandated by CMS-0057-F (Jan 2027).
2️⃣ Data Aggregation: MedCard pulls your complete medical records in real-time. SSRAA identity verification ensures only you authorize access.
3️⃣ Smart Insights: AI detects drug interactions, suggests preventive screenings, and reconciles medications across providers.
4️⃣ Patient Control: Blockchain-verified audit trail logs every access. You see who accessed your data, revoke instantly, and remain in control.
